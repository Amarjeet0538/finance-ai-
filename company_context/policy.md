# CenterAlign Corporate Expense & Travel Policy

Effective Date: 2026-01-01  
Reference Date: 2026-10-13  
Version: 3.2

---

### <!-- clause: P1 --> P1: Proof of Purchase & Receipt Requirement
Receipts or valid tax invoices are mandatory for all expense claims exceeding **₹500**. For expenses of ₹500 or less, self-declaration is permitted only if a physical receipt was unavailable. Claims above ₹500 without an attached receipt identifier or document cannot be processed.

### <!-- clause: P2 --> P2: Daily Meal Allowance
The daily meal allowance limit is **₹1,500 per person per day**. If multiple meal expenses occur on the same calendar day for an employee, their aggregate sum must not exceed ₹1,500 unless pre-approved as client entertainment. Any unapproved excess will be rejected.

### <!-- clause: P3 --> P3: Ground Transportation & Cabs
Local travel, taxi, rideshare (Uber/Ola), and auto-rickshaw expenses are reimbursed up to **₹3,000 per trip or day**. Expenses exceeding this threshold require prior manager justification.

### <!-- clause: P4 --> P4: Air Travel & Flights
All air travel requires prior written manager pre-approval. Employees must obtain written email confirmation from their designated manager citing travel dates and estimated cost prior to booking. Claims submitted without verified manager pre-approval will be held for review.

### <!-- clause: P5 --> P5: Hotel & Lodging
Standard business hotel stays are capped at **₹6,000 per night** (excluding standard local taxes). Stays exceeding this rate require prior approval from the department head.

### <!-- clause: P6 --> P6: Software & Technology Subscriptions
Software licenses, SaaS subscriptions, or digital tooling expenses exceeding **₹5,000** require verified approval from the IT Department. Subscriptions under ₹5,000 may be approved under normal departmental budget.

### <!-- clause: P7 --> P7: Client Entertainment & Team Events
Client entertainment, client dinners, and team events must be explicitly categorized as **Client entertainment** and require managerial approval regardless of amount. Business justification citing attendee names and client organization is mandatory.

### <!-- clause: P8 --> P8: Submission Time Window
All expense claims must be filed within **90 days** of the transaction date. Any claim submitted more than 90 days after the expense occurred is considered stale and ineligible for reimbursement.

### <!-- clause: P9 --> P9: Non-Reimbursable Items (Alcohol & Personal Expenses)
Alcoholic beverages, personal grooming, hotel minibar items, and personal entertainment are strictly non-reimbursable. If a receipt contains alcoholic line items, the claim cannot be automatically approved and is subject to rejection.

### <!-- clause: P10 --> P10: High-Value Claim Auto-Approve Threshold
Regardless of category or pre-existing documentation, any individual claim exceeding **₹10,000** cannot be automatically submitted without explicit human review and approval in the execution loop.

### <!-- clause: P11 --> P11: Duplicate Submissions Prohibition
Submitting duplicate expense claims (defined as identical employee, transaction date, vendor, and amount) is strictly prohibited and treated as a policy violation.
